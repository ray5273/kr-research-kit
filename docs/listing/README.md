# KR Research Kit — Listing Assets

## Summary

An integrated toolkit for Korean and U.S. equity research. Combines DART and SEC filings, analyst reports, charts, and backtesting to produce evidence-based investment analysis, with support for market news summaries and Naver Blog publishing.

## Full Description

KR Research Kit is an integrated skill collection for researching Korean and U.S. equities. It helps AI agents turn filings, market data, analyst coverage, and recent news into structured, dated research with traceable sources.

The toolkit covers the research workflow from scoping a question and gathering evidence to writing company and sector reports, updating existing analysis, and preparing content for publication.

Key capabilities include:

- **Company and sector research:** Investment memos, earnings analysis, peer comparisons, sector reports, and incremental updates.
- **Primary-source analysis:** Structured evidence extraction from Korean DART filings and U.S. SEC filings, including financial results, business segments, cash flows, and risk disclosures.
- **Analyst coverage:** Discovery and summaries of Korean brokerage reports, foreign investment bank views, and independent commentary.
- **Charts and market screening:** Technical chart generation, relative-strength screening, market leadership analysis, and Korean market sentiment indicators.
- **Specialized research:** Order backlog analysis and trade-flow inference for businesses where contracts or exports drive performance.
- **Strategy backtesting:** Reproducible daily-bar backtests for Korean and U.S. equity strategies, with explicit rules, assumptions, and performance records.
- **News and publishing:** Korean-language daily market news reports for Korean and U.S. markets, Naver Blog post preparation, and optional Telegram delivery.

KR Research Kit prioritizes primary sources, records source dates, and separates disclosed facts from interpretation. Its reusable Markdown, JSON, chart, and report artifacts make research easier to review, maintain, and share.
