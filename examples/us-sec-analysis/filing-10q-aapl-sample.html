Apple Inc. Form 10-Q

PART I

Item 1. Financial Statements

The unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements include net sales, gross margin, operating income, net income, diluted earnings per share, assets, liabilities, shareholders' equity, operating cash flow and capital expenditures for the quarter and year-to-date periods.

Item 2. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Management discussed quarterly net sales, product and services performance, gross margin, operating expenses, operating income, other income and expense, income taxes, and liquidity. Services revenue increased year over year, while product revenue reflected customer demand, launch timing, channel inventory and foreign exchange. Gross margin was affected by mix, component costs and services contribution.

Liquidity remained supported by operating cash flow and existing cash and marketable securities. The Company continued to return capital through dividends and share repurchases while funding capital expenditures and working capital. Analysts should tie this discussion to the 10-Q XBRL facts and should not treat a year-to-date cash-flow value as a standalone quarter unless the prior cumulative fact is available.

The MD&A section is used for management explanation, while the financial statements and XBRL facts remain the primary source for formal numbers.

Item 3. Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk

Market risk disclosures discuss foreign currency and interest rate exposures.

Item 4. Controls and Procedures

Management evaluated disclosure controls and procedures as of the end of the quarterly period.

PART II

Item 1. Legal Proceedings

The Company described legal proceedings and contingencies by reference to the notes to the condensed consolidated financial statements.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

There were no material changes from the risk factors disclosed in the most recent Form 10-K, except as updated by the quarterly report. A filing-precision summary should cite this language instead of assuming that no risk exists.

Item 2. Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities and Use of Proceeds

The Company disclosed issuer purchases of equity securities during the quarter.

Item 6. Exhibits

The exhibit index lists documents filed with the quarterly report.
